# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Official release of Mero Miti Flutter date picker and converter ecosystem.
- Ultra-compact 24-bit compressed Bikram Sambat calendar engine (< 1 KB data).
- Bidirectional AD <-> BS date conversion (1973 to 2099 BS).
- Full Nepali Fiscal Year (आर्थिक वर्ष) support and 4 financial quarters calculations.
- Bilingual localization (Devanagari and English numerals/month names).
- Modern Material 3 `MeroMitiTextField` and bottom sheet date picker dialog.
- Runtime dynamic year registration (`registerYear`).
